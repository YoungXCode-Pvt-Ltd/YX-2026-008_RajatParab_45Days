### Critical Challenges Identified

| Problem | Impact | Metric |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Delayed deliveries | Client dissatisfaction, penalties | 75% projects late |
| Low collection rate | Working capital blockage | 54.3% collected |
| Overdue invoices | Rs2.5M at risk | 11 invoices overdue |
| Budget overruns | Margin erosion | 13% projects over budget |
| Workload imbalance | Burnout risk, inefficiency | 100.4% vs 68% utilization |

---

### Strategic Recommendations

**Days 1-30 (Quick Wins)**
- Deploy Streamlit analytics dashboard
- Collection drive for overdue invoices
- Workload rebalancing across team
- Fast-track/delay 8 delayed projects

**Days 31-60 (Process Improvements)**
- Milestone tracking for all projects
- Standardized project estimation
- Automated invoice reminders
- Monthly capacity planning

**Days 61-90 (Strategic)**
- Client re-engagement campaign
- Quarterly business reviews
- Change request governance
- Revenue forecasting model
